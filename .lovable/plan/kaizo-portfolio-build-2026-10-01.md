# KAIZO Portfolio Build

## Goal
Build the complete one-page KAIZO portfolio from the supplied brief, replacing the blank template and all old studio positioning with a personal, first-person identity.

## What I’ll build
- Sticky editorial navigation with KAIZO avatar, desktop links, full-screen mobile menu, and prominent WhatsApp access.
- A cinematic scrapbook-style opening with the uploaded portrait as the main visual, taped-photo treatment, live timecode, status, and contact actions.
- A film-contact-sheet work section using all supplied Vimeo projects, with lazy-loaded players and correct vertical/horizontal ratios.
- Personal About section, three receipt-style pricing offers, hand-drawn three-step process, strong contact section, floating WhatsApp/Instagram actions, and minimal footer.
- Mobile-first layouts, keyboard access, reduced-motion support, visible focus states, and restrained editorial animation.
- KAIZO favicon, title, description, social metadata, and Person structured data.

## Visual direction
- Dominant ink black and paper cream, with restrained Goku orange and deep blue accents.
- Anton display type, DM Sans body type, Caveat notes, and JetBrains Mono labels.
- Taped photographs, torn edges, marker strokes, halftone, film grain, crop marks, hand-drawn arrows, slight rotations, and one-editor details.
- No stock media, agency language, fake metrics, testimonials, generic gradients, or template-style feature grids.

## Technical notes
- Keep this as one fast page with smooth section navigation.
- Centralize all contact and social links so every CTA stays consistent.
- Use the supplied image from the project asset service and a small optimized copy for the favicon.
- Vimeo media will load only near the viewport or after interaction to reduce initial page weight.
- Verify the finished page at desktop and mobile sizes, including menus, links, overflow, and media layout.
